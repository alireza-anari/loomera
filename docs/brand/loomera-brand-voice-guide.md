# Loomera Brand Voice Guide

> Version: 1.0  
> Brand: Loomera  
> Purpose: Source-ready voice system for product, marketing, support, and partner communications.

---

## 1) Brand Voice Core

### Brand Personality
Loomera is calm, refined, trustworthy, thoughtful, precise, and human.

The brand should feel:
- composed, never cold
- premium, never exclusionary
- helpful, never robotic
- clear, never flat
- warm, never overly casual
- elegant, never decorative

### Voice Essence
**Calm confidence for better self-care decisions.**

### Core Brand Traits
- Calm
- Trusted
- Clear
- Refined
- Thoughtful
- Reassuring

### Brand Promise
Loomera helps people discover, choose, and book beauty and wellness services with more clarity, more trust, and less friction.

### Brand Tone Summary
Loomera speaks with calm confidence. It helps people make decisions without pressure, move through booking with clarity, and feel reassured at every step. Its tone is polished but never distant, premium but always accessible, and human without becoming casual or noisy.

---

## 2) Tone Dimensions

### warm ↔ formal
**Position:** slightly warm, controlled  
Loomera should feel human and respectful, not stiff or bureaucratic.

### elegant ↔ casual
**Position:** elegant  
The brand should feel tasteful and considered, not chatty or informal.

### calm ↔ energetic
**Position:** calm  
Loomera guides with steadiness and reassurance rather than excitement.

### premium ↔ mass-market
**Position:** premium but accessible  
The language should feel elevated and clean, without sounding exclusive.

### supportive ↔ instructional
**Position:** balanced, leaning supportive  
The user should feel guided, not managed.

### minimal ↔ expressive
**Position:** minimal with warmth  
Language should stay concise, but never feel blunt.

### human ↔ technical
**Position:** human-first  
Even system messages should feel written for people, not processes.

---

## 3) Voice Principles

### 1. Reassure before you persuade
Before asking the user to act, reduce uncertainty.

### 2. Clarity is part of care
Being clear is not only functional; it is part of the brand’s feeling of trust.

### 3. Premium comes from restraint
Loomera sounds refined through precision, calmness, and good judgment—not through flashy language.

### 4. Beauty language should feel modern, never cliché
Avoid tired beauty tropes, exaggerated promises, and over-feminine phrasing.

### 5. Helpful, not mechanical
The brand should explain and guide in a way that feels attentive and considerate.

### 6. Every sentence should reduce friction
Copy should make the next step easier, faster, and more understandable.

### 7. Human, but never overfamiliar
Warmth should come through respect and care, not exaggerated friendliness.

## 4) Do / Don’t Rules

### Rule 1 — Be clear, not vague
**Explanation:** Users should immediately understand what something means and what to do next.

**Bad:**
> تجربه‌ای دل‌انگیز در انتظار شماست.

**Better:**
> سالن، متخصص و زمان مناسب را در چند قدم انتخاب کنید.

### Rule 2 — Be reassuring, not pushy
**Explanation:** Loomera should invite action without pressure.

**Bad:**
> همین حالا رزرو کن و شگفت‌زده شو!

**Better:**
> زمان مناسب خود را انتخاب کنید و رزرو را نهایی کنید.

### Rule 3 — Sound premium, not pretentious
**Explanation:** Quality should come through polish and control.

**Bad:**
> این تجربه برای سلیقه‌های خاص و متفاوت طراحی شده است.

**Better:**
> برای انتخابی دقیق‌تر و تجربه‌ای باکیفیت‌تر.

### Rule 4 — Be human, not robotic
**Explanation:** Especially in payment, booking, support, and errors.

**Bad:**
> درخواست شما با خطا مواجه شد.

**Better:**
> پرداخت کامل نشد. مبلغی کسر نشده است. لطفاً دوباره تلاش کنید.

### Rule 5 — Guide, don’t command
**Explanation:** The tone should support action, not police it.

**Bad:**
> اطلاعات را صحیح وارد کنید.

**Better:**
> برای ادامه، شماره موبایل را با فرمت درست وارد کنید.

### Rule 6 — Avoid beauty clichés
**Explanation:** Do not lean on exaggerated, gendered, or superficial language.

**Bad:**
> درخشش زنانه‌ات را آزاد کن.

**Better:**
> خدمتی را انتخاب کنید که با ترجیح و زمان شما هماهنگ باشد.

### Rule 7 — Keep it short, but not abrupt
**Explanation:** Brevity should still feel complete.

**Bad:**
> ثبت شد.

**Better:**
> نوبت شما ثبت شد.

## 5) Writing Style Rules

### Sentence Length
- Prefer short to medium-length sentences.
- Product UI: 2–8 words when possible.
- Helper text: 1 short sentence.
- Marketing copy: 1 main line + 1 supporting line.

### User Address
- In Persian, address users respectfully.
- Use **شما**.
- Do not use **تو** in product, support, or transactional contexts.

### Word Choice
- Use simple, polished, everyday words.
- Favor precision over flourish.
- Avoid heavy administrative language.
- Avoid theatrical or poetic abstraction.

### CTA Writing
- Use direct, action-led language.
- Name the action clearly.
- Prefer “رزرو نوبت” over “شروع تجربه”.

### Helper Text Writing
- Clarify what is needed or what happens next.
- Do not repeat the label.
- Keep it specific.

### Microcopy Style
- One idea per line.
- Reduce ambiguity.
- Keep tone consistent with the action.

### Punctuation Style
- Use punctuation lightly.
- Avoid excessive exclamation marks.
- Avoid ellipses in UI.
- Avoid emoji in product copy.
- In marketing, punctuation should still stay restrained.

### Tone in Sensitive Situations
Use this order:
1. acknowledge
2. clarify impact
3. offer next step

Example:
> پرداخت کامل نشد. مبلغی کسر نشده است. لطفاً دوباره تلاش کنید.

### Error Message Tone
Use this structure:
**What happened + what it means + what to do next**

### Empty State Tone
- Never blame the user.
- Keep it calm.
- Offer a useful next action.

### Support Tone
- Empathetic
- practical
- accountable
- calm

### Payment / Booking Tone
- precise
- reassuring
- status-led
- easy to scan

## 6) Context-Based Voice

### A. Landing / Marketing
Tone becomes slightly more expressive and aspirational, but still calm and controlled.

**Goal:** build trust, communicate ease, express quality.

### B. Product UI
Tone becomes shorter, more functional, and more immediate.

**Goal:** reduce friction and support decisions.

### C. Booking Flow
Tone becomes step-based, supportive, and confidence-building.

**Goal:** keep users oriented and reassured.

### D. Checkout / Payment
Tone becomes highly precise and explicit.

**Goal:** reduce uncertainty and make status crystal clear.

### E. Empty States
Tone becomes gently corrective and helpful.

**Goal:** reopen the path forward.

### F. Notifications / Reminders
Tone becomes concise, useful, and respectful of attention.

**Goal:** inform without sounding intrusive.

### G. Support / Help Center
Tone becomes more empathetic and explanatory.

**Goal:** reduce stress and guide toward resolution.

### H. Legal / Privacy / Terms
Tone becomes more formal and exact, but still readable.

**Goal:** make important information understandable, not intimidating.

### I. Partner-facing / Salon Onboarding
Tone becomes more structured and commercially confident.

**Goal:** communicate quality, reliability, and operational value.

## 7) CTA Style Guide

### CTA Rules by Type

#### Primary CTA
Use for the main action.
- direct
- short
- outcome-oriented

Examples:
- رزرو نوبت
- ادامه
- تأیید و ادامه
- پرداخت و ثبت نهایی

#### Secondary CTA
Use for lower-priority or parallel actions.

Examples:
- مشاهده جزئیات
- بازگشت
- مشاهده زمان‌های دیگر
- بررسی بیشتر

#### Supportive CTA
Use to reduce uncertainty or help the user choose.

Examples:
- راهنمای انتخاب
- تماس با پشتیبانی
- مشاهده شرایط

#### Empty State CTA
Use to reopen momentum.

Examples:
- جست‌وجوی جدید
- تغییر فیلترها
- همه خدمات را ببینید

#### Alert / Modal CTA
Use simple, decision-led language.

Examples:
- متوجه شدم
- دوباره تلاش می‌کنم
- لغو
- تأیید

#### Onboarding CTA
Use low-friction, progress-friendly language.

Examples:
- شروع
- پروفایل را کامل کنید
- شهر خود را انتخاب کنید

#### Support CTA
Use problem-solving language.

Examples:
- ثبت درخواست
- مشاهده پاسخ‌های رایج
- گفت‌وگو با پشتیبانی

## 8) Microcopy Library Direction

### Buttons
- action-first
- short
- explicit

### Badges
- short
- status-led
- easily scannable

Examples:
- جدید
- محبوب
- در انتظار
- تأیید شد

### Filter Labels
- precise
- neutral
- category-based

Examples:
- زمان
- قیمت
- نوع خدمت
- امتیاز

### Search Helper Text
Should tell users what they can search for.

Example:
> نام سالن، خدمت یا متخصص را جست‌وجو کنید.

### No Results Messages
Should stay calm and useful.

Example:
> نتیجه‌ای با این فیلترها پیدا نشد. می‌توانید زمان، قیمت یا نوع خدمت را تغییر دهید.

### Booking Status Labels
- رزرو شد
- در انتظار تأیید
- لغو شد
- انجام شد

### Payment Status Labels
- پرداخت شد
- در انتظار پرداخت
- ناموفق
- بازگشت وجه انجام شد

### Profile / Account Labels
- نوبت‌های من
- اطلاعات حساب
- روش‌های پرداخت
- اعلان‌ها
- تنظیمات

### Trust / Reassurance Lines
- جزئیات رزرو قبل از پرداخت نهایی نمایش داده می‌شود.
- زمان و اطلاعات رزرو پس از ثبت در دسترس شما خواهد بود.
- در صورت نیاز، می‌توانید پیش از نهایی‌سازی جزئیات را بررسی کنید.

### Confirmation Messages
Structure:
1. clear success state
2. essential detail
3. next point of access

Example:
> نوبت شما ثبت شد. جزئیات رزرو در همین صفحه و پیامک تأیید در دسترس است.

### Reminder Messages
Keep reminders useful and concise.

Example:
> یادآوری: نوبت ماساژ شما فردا ساعت ۱۷:۳۰ است.

## 9) Vocabulary System

### Preferred Words
- انتخاب
- رزرو
- زمان
- جزئیات
- تأیید
- خدمات
- متخصص
- مناسب
- روشن
- با اطمینان
- دقیق
- تجربه
- هماهنگ
- قابل اعتماد
- رسیدگی

### Words to Avoid
- شگفت‌انگیز
- فوق‌العاده
- جادویی
- بی‌نظیر
- لاکچری
- خاص‌ترین
- درخشش زنانه
- تحول‌آفرین
- unbelievable
- best ever

### Brand-Friendly Alternatives
- بهترین → مناسب‌تر
- فوق‌العاده → باکیفیت
- سریع و آسان → ساده و روشن
- جادویی → آرام و دلنشین
- لاکچری → premium / باکیفیت

### Tone-Safe Phrases
- با اطمینان انتخاب کنید
- جزئیات را بررسی کنید
- زمان مناسب خود را پیدا کنید
- نوبت شما ثبت شد
- هنوز گزینه‌های دیگری هم هست
- هر زمان خواستید می‌توانید تغییر دهید

### Phrases That Feel Too Generic / Too Salesy / Too Cliché
- بهترین انتخاب شما
- تجربه‌ای متفاوت و بی‌نظیر
- زیبایی را دوباره تعریف کنید
- بدرخشید
- خاص‌ترین شما
- جادوی زیبایی
- همین حالا شگفت‌زده شوید

## 10) Persian Voice Guidance

### Overall Persian Tone
Persian copy should be natural, elegant, readable, and respectful.
It should not feel translated, overly literary, or too colloquial.

### Recommended Formality Level
**Polished semi-formal**

This means:
- respectful but not stiff
- warm but not intimate
- premium but still everyday-readable

### How to Keep the Tone Premium and Human at the Same Time
Use:
- clean structure
- measured vocabulary
- short sentences
- calm confidence
- respectful phrasing

Avoid:
- ornamental phrasing
- luxury clichés
- exaggerated emotion
- slang

### Suitable Beauty / Wellness Vocabulary
Preferred semantic fields:
- آرامش
- مراقبت
- رسیدگی
- انتخاب
- کیفیت
- تجربه
- هماهنگ
- مناسب
- اطمینان
- زمان شما
- ترجیح شما

### Expressions to Avoid in Persian
- خوشگل‌تر شو
- بدرخش
- جذاب‌تر از همیشه
- زیبایی زنانه‌ات
- لاکچری
- خاصِ خاص
- معجزه
- بی‌رقیب
- عشقم / عزیزم / دوست خوبم

## 14) Short Handoff Version

### For Content Team
Write with clarity first, elegance second. The brand may inspire, but it should never oversell. Avoid beauty clichés and exaggerated language.

### For Product Team
Keep microcopy short, explicit, and friction-reducing. Every label, helper text, and message should help the user understand what is happening and what comes next.

### For Support Team
Start with empathy, then explain clearly, then offer the next step. Never imply that the user is at fault. Stay calm and practical.

### For Marketing Team
Build trust before desire. Speak with polish and confidence, but avoid sounding glossy, loud, or generic.

### For Partner / Salon Team
Use a more structured, operational tone while keeping Loomera’s clarity and refinement. Emphasize reliability, professionalism, and quality of experience.
