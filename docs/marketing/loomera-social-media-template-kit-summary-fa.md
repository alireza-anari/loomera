# Loomera Social Media Template Kit — Summary Ready

## خلاصه‌ی یک‌صفحه‌ای

### direction نهایی
برای Loomera بهترین social template system، **refined product-marketing hybrid** است: 
- content-forward
- softly branded
- product-aware
- calm and trusted

### core rules
- **Palette:** light-first, violet-led, sage-accent, low-noise
- **Typography:** Yekan Bakh / Vazirmatn, RTL-first, short readable hierarchy
- **Logo:** restrained, mostly glyph-cue, full logo فقط در trust-heavy covers
- **Copy:** direct but calm, tasteful, non-hype, evidence-aware
- **Visual behavior:** rounded, spacious, modular, softly branded

### familyهای اصلی
- Brand Awareness
- Category / Service Highlight
- Provider Spotlight
- Educational / Tips
- Trust / Review
- Offer / Campaign
- Product Feature / Booking Simplicity
- Launch / Update
- Story
- Carousel
- Partner Recruitment

### anti-cliché rules
- no neon beauty palette
- no pink-only / gold luxury clichés
- no stock-spa stereotype visuals
- no exaggerated claims
- no oversized logo stamping
- no salesy urgency theater

### approve checklist
- brand-consistent?
- readable on mobile?
- calm + trusted tone preserved?
- token-based color use?
- reusable layout?
- product-reality aligned?
- logo usage controlled?

### asset output
- square templates
- story templates
- carousel cover + inner
- partner recruitment
- light-first masters + optional dark variants
